# ISM Brand Guidelines, Email-Relevant Extract (v. June 2026)

©2025 International Scripture Ministries, Inc. Proprietary. This extract distills the full ISM Brand Guidelines into what governs email production. Where this file and `email-standard.md` differ, the **email standard wins** for email-specific rules (backgrounds, CTA color, subject length). This file governs voice, editorial style, typography names, and messaging.

## 1. Who ISM Is (What / How / Why)

- **What:** We distribute Bibles to all languages of the world.
- **How:** We use technology, mobile apps, radio programs, digital and social media, meeting people wherever they already are in their language.
- **Why:** The Bible is the inspired Word of God, it transforms lives when experienced in one's own language, and Jesus Christ Himself commissioned His followers.

**Category:** FaithTech (primary), with Digital Ministry and Global Outreach Technology as secondary. Position ISM as a tech leader in the Christian sphere, akin to how SpaceX/Tesla represent innovation.

## 2. Messaging Statements (use in email framing, footers, campaigns)

- **Tagline:** Making Scripture accessible to all nations, anywhere.
- **Mission (why):** "We leverage cutting-edge technology to deliver unhindered access to the Bible, ensuring every person can experience the transformative power of God's Word."
- **Vision (what):** "A world where every individual on earth has unhindered access to the Bible, God's Holy Scriptures."
- **Positioning (how):** "We are the only organization at the forefront of Technology for Global Bible Access, pioneering AI-driven technologies and broadcast network spectrums to make Scripture accessible across all barriers, political, cultural, big tech, and more."
- **Distinctive Value (secret sauce):** "We are the only FaithTech organization pioneering cutting-edge technology to create a global Bible network that transcends all barriers."

## 3. Voice & Tone

**Primary Tone, Mission-Driven Guide (Hero archetype), ~80% of the time.** Bold, clear, and compassionate, calling others into transformative engagement with God's Word. Pillars:
- Purposeful Empowerment, inspire confidence and hope.
- Inclusive Access, respect diverse backgrounds, languages, cultures.
- Bold Transformation, highlight the transformative power of God's Word.
- Direct and Honest, straightforward, mission-true.
- Driven by Mission, convey urgency of reaching every language, tribe, and tongue.
- Empathetic Understanding, compassion and respect for those served.

**Secondary Tone, Visionary Creator (Creator archetype), ~15-20% of the time.** Future-focused, imaginative, inspiring. Use for educational/inspirational emails: Boundless Innovation, Artful Storytelling, Visionary Hope, Purposeful Creativity, Inspiring Collaboration.

Every message should inspire hope and reflect belief in unhindered access to Scripture. Prioritize brevity and clarity (say more with less). Communicate with cultural respect, avoid slang, jargon, or metaphors that will not translate.

## 4. Editorial Style (apply to all email copy)

- **Voice:** Active voice ("ISM launched the app," not "The app was launched by ISM").
- **Abbreviations:** Spell out first reference, then acronym if familiar. Spell out Bible references (Revelation 1:1, not Rev. 1:1).
- **Titles:** Italicize titles of books, media, studies (or quotation marks if italics unavailable).
- **Dates:** "Tuesday, March 9." No suffixes (th, rd), no abbreviations, no year unless needed.
- **Email & Web in body text:** Italicize (e.g., *troy@ism.bible*, *ism.bible*). Do not underline or use "www."
- **Phone numbers:** Periods, e.g., 214.494.1147 x105. Never publish personal numbers. Exception: the ministry line is always written `1-833-60-BIBLE (24253)` (see `email-standard.md` §8).
- **Time:** AM/PM in caps, no periods. Ranges: 7–8:30 PM.
- **Capitalization:** Avoid all caps. Capitalize deity pronouns (He, Him, His). Capitalize Scripture, Gospel (biblical book), New Testament, names of Deity.
- **Commas:** Oxford comma in lists.
- **Punctuation:** One space after periods. Commas and periods go inside quotes.
- **Numbers:** Spell out one through nine; numerals for 10 and above (six-week series, 10-language release).
- **Exclamation points:** Use sparingly, never more than two in a row.
- **Word choices:** Regardless (not irregardless); Ensure (not insure unless actual insurance); It's/Its correctly; Effect (noun)/Affect (verb).

## 5. Brand Name Style

- **Dot Bible**, clean, readable version for headlines, body copy, marketing (default in email copy).
- **dot.Bible**, digital/domain/UI contexts and URLs (e.g., "You can download Dot Bible at *dot.Bible*").

## 6. Visual Identity for Email

### Color Palette (full)
**Primary:** Solar Black `#000000`, Steadfast Slate `#41484E`, Celestial White `#F4F4F6`, Horizon Blue `#046DE3`, Radiant Dawn `#E7B650`.
**Secondary:** Muted Steel `#6D7680`, Bright Ivory `#FFFFE8`, Ocean Mist `#A3D7FF`, Golden Glow `#F5C878`.

For email, apply the six-color **Approved Email Palette** in `email-standard.md` §6. Reserve Horizon Blue for buttons/links; Radiant Dawn for accents/dividers only.

### Typography
- **Poppins**, headings. Bold for H1-H3, Semi-Bold for H4-H6. (Email fallback stack: `'Poppins', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`.)
- **Nunito**, body text, regular, 14-16px. (Fallback: `'Nunito', Arial, sans-serif`.)
- **Lora**, stylized captions/quotes, italic or bold. (Fallback: `'Lora', Georgia, serif`.)

Note: Custom web fonts render inconsistently across email clients. Always include the fallback stacks above so the email degrades gracefully.

### Iconography
Flat, minimalist, line-based icons with a modern aesthetic, aligned to the palette, clear and universally understood.

### Imagery
High-quality, minimalist, emotionally resonant. **Do:** authentic, relatable imagery; high contrast and focus. **Don't:** generic or impersonal stock imagery. Every email image needs accurate alt text (per the standard).
