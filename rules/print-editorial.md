# Print and editorial

Source: the ISM Partnership Guide, Donor Impact In Motion and Sojourner Media Kit brochures, reproduced by `reference/templates/ism-brochure` and `reference/templates/ism-impact-report`. Values below are read from those templates and the overview, not invented. Machine-readable copy: `rules.json` under `applied.print`.

## Page
- Letter, 8.5 × 11 in, portrait, about 0.6 in margins (56px in the template), white stock. Cover and closing page invert to `#0E1013`.
- The impact report adds a 0.125 in bleed (11 pages) and exports to print-ready PDF.
- Alternate dark and off-white pages for rhythm. Full-bleed photography takes a whole column or a page-foot band, never a floated inset.

## Section opener
- Boxed eyebrow: uppercase, 11px, weight 700, 0.2em tracking, Horizon Blue text (`#1a7fe8` on white, `#2A86F0` on dark) inside a 1px blue rectangle. Never a pill. A 1px blue rule runs from the box to the page edge. Every section opens this way.
- Impact report and media kit: add a two-digit index (`01`, `02`) beside the title.

## Headlines and body
- Headline: Inter 600, 31–54px, -0.02em tracking, near-black on white and white on dark. One key word may take the gradient marker highlight `linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(75,110,224,.45), rgba(143,125,224,.45))`.
- Subline: bold italic, 14–15px, `#1a7fe8`, directly under the headline.
- Body: 12.5px / 1.65, justified with hyphenation, two columns. Bold lead-ins inside bullets.
- Pull quote: right-aligned, 23px weight 600, one phrase in violet `#8f7de0`.
- Numbered next steps: blue bold italic (`1. Meet with Our Team`) with justified body below.
- Faces: Inter for text. Procerus, Neue Power and Miss Noteworthy are accent faces only; never body copy.

## Graphic elements
- Icon medallions: 62px gradient circles (blue, deep blue, gold, lavender), white line icon (28px, black file inverted), 5px white halo ring.
- Closing band: full-bleed gradient (`linear-gradient(100deg,#4b6ee0,#8f7de0 45%,#e8b98a)`) carrying one centred white statement.
- Stat rows: four-up gradient numerals. Unconfirmed figures stay as literal `[ # ]` or `TBD`. Never invent numbers.
- Inset callouts: black panels. Back cover: CTA plus a QR block.
- Logo: small white iSM mark bottom-right of every inner page (38px tall in the brochure; the black file inverted). Never redraw it.

## Boilerplate
- Footer line on the closing page, 10px, low emphasis: `International Scripture Ministries, Inc. | PO Box 24253 Boone, IA 50950 | +1 (833) 60-24253 | ISM.Bible`.
- Canonical self-description: "a FaithTech organization dedicated to delivering unhindered access to the Bible so every person can know Christ through God's Word." Reuse verbatim.
- Media-kit modules pair a QR code with a plain-text URL.

## Never
- Pill eyebrows, centred body copy, a plain grey rule where a gradient hairline belongs, redrawn logos, invented statistics.

## Open items
Print point sizes were read from templates and text flow, not measured from the PDFs. Verify against a press proof before relying on them for a new format.
