# Slide decks

Source: TAP Deck v2 and CFNI Presentation Deck 2026, reproduced slide for slide by `reference/templates/cfni-tap-deck`. Machine-readable copy: `rules.json` under `applied.decks`.

## Canvas and colour
- 1920 × 1080. Slide field is near-black `#16191E`, not pure black. Light slides use `#f4f3f1`. Section dividers use a full-bleed gradient mesh (`deck-bg-gradient-dark.jpg`, `deck-bg-gradient-light.jpg`).
- One accent colour per slide. Blue is the action and subtitle colour; amber `#F3B25A` is the statistic colour.

## Anatomy
- Headline: white, 66–76px, weight 700, -0.02em, with a blue italic subtitle directly beneath (`#2A86F0`, 30–34px, weight 700). This pair is the deck signature and appears on nearly every content slide.
- Layout: left text column, right full-bleed photo, split about 56/44. The photo bleeds to the slide edge and carries a blue to violet to peach gradient wash at its lower corner.
- Dot mark: a 2–3 dot cluster (one outlined) above the headline, top-left.
- Body: 25–28px; lead paragraphs `rgba(255,255,255,.86)`, supporting text `.62–.72`. Plain disc bullets.
- Hairlines: 1px `linear-gradient(90deg,#4b6ee0,#8f7de0,#f3b25a)` between stacked label and description rows. Never a plain grey rule.
- Stats: amber numerals 96px or larger in cards with a hairline border, `rgba(255,255,255,.03)` fill and 20px radius.
- Icon chips: 76–78px circles in a two-stop slice of the signature gradient with a white icon.
- Section dividers: one centred headline, no body copy.
- Gradient panel opener: half-slide gradient card with product name and italic tagline, photo on the other half, small gradient bar plus white line bottom-left.
- Closing slide: "To Reach Billions, We Need **You**." with one gradient-clipped word.
- Logo: small white iSM mark bottom-right, about 58px, on every slide.

## Archetype slides (11)
Cover, split text and photo, labelled benefits, process steps, light slide, gradient divider, gradient panel opener, stats, testimonial, icon grid, closing call to action.

## Never
Outlined eyebrow pills, centred body copy, drop shadows on cards, more than one accent colour on a slide.
