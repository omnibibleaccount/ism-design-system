# Social

Status: thin. The only source is the Instagram grid board (`packages/web/assets/imagery/Social-Instagram-Grid.jpg`), the overview's accent-face notes and the `ISM-Looks-*` boards. Everything here is observed, not measured. Nothing in this file is a pixel spec. Machine-readable copy: `rules.json` under `applied.social`. Ask the ISM design owner before treating any line as final.

## What the grid shows
- Profile: black ISM mark on a white circle, bio "Bringing the Bible beyond barriers so every person can know Christ."
- Posts are portrait tiles that alternate three treatments: dark charcoal typographic cards, cream or pale cards with large type over a photo, and black and white or desaturated documentary photography.
- Big, heavy, wide display type (Neue Power) in amber or white, sometimes with a blue to violet to gold gradient fill, often cropped or stacked ("UNTIL NOW", "IN SECRET UNDER THREAT", "GOD IS OUR REFUGE", "WORLD").
- Handwritten accent lines (Miss Noteworthy) in gold for a human aside ("They still share the Bible").
- Small tracked uppercase captions (Inter) for context ("FOR GOD SO LOVED THE WORLD", "DROPPING SOON").
- Halftone dot patterns and thin concentric circle marks used as texture, small and in corners or fading across a photo. Photos are often cropped to a circle.
- Verse cards set one verse in several scripts at once.

## Rules to apply
- Display and script faces are social and campaign accents only. Never in product UI.
- Keep one accent colour per tile (amber or white, or the gradient). Blue remains the action colour.
- Real photography of people, phones, books and global settings. No generic staged church stock.
- Verse text is never altered; show reference and translation (see `voice-microcopy.md`). Set `lang` and use the right font for each script when showing several scripts.
- Patterns small, never all-over.
