02 Stories from the field

Lives changed

The impact of Scripture is more of a trickle effect at first.

Dallas, Texas

Troy still tells the story of a woman he met in a Dallas coffee shop who speaks Waray, a language four and a half million people call home. He handed her a Bible in her own language. Twenty-four hours later, twenty people in her circle had one too.

Cambodia

That same pattern showed up again this year in Cambodia. A missionary named Hounam handed her friend Reaksmey Khema a TAP card, a small card that opens the full Bible in audio with one tap of a phone. Within days, Khema was opening it every morning before anything else.

"She told Hounam it felt like attending Bible school."

When barriers come down, Scripture has room to work. Six months into 2026, that work continues every day because of partners like you.

05 Ambassadors & TAP reach

55,000 cards, 86 countries, 112 languages

So far in 2026, 55,000 cards have reached people across 86 countries, opening Scripture in 112 languages — including 691 taps in Khmer, the language Khema reads. Of those cards, 8,460 have led to an active tap this year: a listener opening the Bible again somewhere in the world.
