Who We Are

“Our mission is simple yet bold: to ensure every person, everywhere, has unhindered access to Scripture.”

International Scripture Ministries (ISM) is a global faith and technology organization dedicated to delivering unhindered access to the Bible, enabling every person, everywhere, on every platform to know Christ.

In 2019, our founder, Troy Carl, followed a vision God placed in his heart to “take the Bible up there” so that every person could access the Word of God. Out of that calling, ISM was established to help fulfill the Great Commission using the tools of our time.

Through innovation, partnerships, and the faithfulness of the global Church, we are bringing Scripture to life in thousands of languages using technology people already have in their hands.

To ensure every person, everywhere, has unhindered access to Scripture. Delivered in their language, through the technologies they already use.

But here's the truth: this vision cannot be accomplished alone. The real heroes are churches and organizations like yours. When you partner with us, you multiply impact scaling innovative platforms like Bible apps, satellite networks, and radio broadcasts that break political, cultural, and technological barriers.

The Problem

Billions still live without access to the Bible in their own language.

Others have God's Word but long for deeper discipleship resources.

We see the challenges every day:

  • Language barriers that prevent people from hearing Scripture in the way their hearts understand.
  • Technological barriers that leave entire regions disconnected from online resources.
  • Cultural barriers that make even translated Bibles difficult to relate to.
  • Political and religious opposition that keeps God's Word out of reach.
  • Discipleship gaps where access exists, but growth resources do not.

These barriers aren't statistics. They're people, families, nations. We exist to break them down so that every person, in every place, can encounter the life-transforming Word of God.

ISM Ministry Streams

dot.Bible Apps

Scripture in Every Language, Right at Your Fingertips.

Our Bible apps deliver Scripture and discipleship resources in hundreds of languages, making it easy for people to read, listen, and share the Word of God. Whether for personal study, small groups, or outreach, these apps put the Bible directly in people's hands.

ISM Publishing

Gospel-Centered Resources that Transform Lives.

We equip people in every season of faith with biblically grounded resources—from Bibles to study guides to devotionals. Through publishing partnerships and platforms like Amazon, we make sure gospel-centered truth is available across languages and nations.

TAP Cards

Share the Bible with a Simple Tap.

Our NFC-powered TAP Cards let anyone instantly share Scripture in hundreds of languages. They are simple, trackable, and perfect for churches, mission teams, or personal evangelism—transforming everyday encounters into opportunities for the gospel.

ISM Radio

Bringing the Word to the Airwaves.

Through HD, satellite, and streaming broadcasts, we deliver the Bible 24/7—even where apps cannot go. Churches and partners use ISM Radio to reach immigrants, refugees, and diaspora communities with the gospel in their heart language.

Each of these streams exists to give partners like you powerful, scalable ways to reach the unreached. Together, we can transform the way the world encounters God's Word.
