How can I get involved?

How do NFC Bible cards work?

Hold the card near the top back of an unlocked phone. The dot.Bible app or website opens to the passage printed on the card.

Can my station carry ISM Radio?

Yes. We supply Scripture readings for HD Radio sub-channels in many languages. Talk to our team to start.

Can I read without internet?

Many apps in the dot.Bible directory work offline once downloaded. Look for the Offline label.
