Statement of Faith

God

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

Deuteronomy 6:4 · Matthew 28:19

Jesus Christ

We believe that Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man. He was born of a virgin, lived without sin, died for our sins and rose bodily from the dead.

“In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.” John 1:1 BSB

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit lives in every believer, giving understanding of the Scriptures and power to live them.
